Customer loyalty

Turn customer feedback into focused action

Your customers form opinions about your organisation as they interact with your products and services, and through key touch points such as service and support. The combination of these experiences impacts the overall relationship with your customers and their likelihood to repurchase or refer.

If you are waiting for clients to complain, rather than soliciting their positive or negative opinion, you may not get the feedback until it is too late.

At Shaping a Vision, we help organisations by using Net Promoter® Score, a simple but highly effective metric. The output of a Net Promoter Score is a clear measure of your organisation’s performance as seen through your customers eyes.

Having this proactive feedback helps you target key improvement areas and customer segments.

  • Net Promoter Score programme setup
  • Management reporting and dashboards
  • Customer feedback analysis
  • Customer service culture development
